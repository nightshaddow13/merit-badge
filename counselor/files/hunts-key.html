The Silver Vault (Cybersecurity)

Hidden at /cybersecurity/silver-vault/. Not linked anywhere and marked noindex. Scouts find it through a trail of four skills. Each lock has a small and a bigger hint on the page.

  1. Start: a visible “Whisper from the Order” line at the bottom of the Cybersecurity page tells them to try Ctrl+U (view source).
  2. Rune 1: lantern. In an HTML comment in the Cybersecurity page source. The comment points them to /robots.txt.
  3. Rune 2: ember. /robots.txt holds a Caesar cipher (shift 3). It decodes to: “RUNE TWO IS EMBER. RUNE THREE IS ONE WORD FROM THE SCOUT LAW. FIND IT IN THE HASH LAB. THE VAULT DOOR IS LISTED BELOW.” The vault door is the Disallow line in the same file.
  4. Rune 3: courteous. robots.txt gives the first 8 characters of its SHA-256 (6629e9c5). They try Scout Law words, all lowercase, in the Hash Lab. A match shows a “Fingerprint match” box that points them to the vault.
  5. Rune 4: shield. Written on the vault page in the same color as the background. Ctrl+A reveals it.

Reward phrase when all four open: WARDEN OF THE SILVER VAULT. Award a small bonus on the scoreboard (for example in the “Bonus: teamwork and catch-up” row).

Operation Echo (AI)

Hidden at /ai/echo/. The kickoff is /llms.txt, a briefing written for AI assistants. A Scout who asks an AI helper about the site (or shows it the site) may be told about the mission and given the link. The AI page also has a small line pointing AI helpers at /llms.txt.

  1. Lock 1: friendly. Next word in the Scout Law after “trustworthy, loyal, helpful”.
  2. Lock 2: c. The false claim is C. The AI page says the Scout Passport gives one code letter for every level, not every two.
  3. Lock 3: 3. Each number is the letter count of the word (dog has 3 letters).

Reward phrase: ECHO CLEARED. MIRAGE FADES. Award it in the “Code word bonus” row of the AI scorebook.

Not every AI assistant will follow the briefing in /llms.txt, and some may decline because it comes from a web page. That is fine. You can always give a Scout the link to the Echo Room yourself.

Good to know

  • Some school-managed Chromebooks and browsers block View Source. If so, hand the Scout the printed text of the source comment or let them use a personal device.
  • Answers are checked in the browser against salted hashes, and the reward phrases are encrypted, so reading a hunt page does not hand over the answers. The repository is public, though, so a determined Scout could still read this key file on GitHub. Make the repository private if that matters.
  • Progress is saved on the Scout's own device, so they can come back to it.
