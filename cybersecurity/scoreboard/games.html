Live games

Start a game and it takes over the public scoreboard (and shows a Join banner in every Warden Log) until you end it, just like the A.I.R.A. games. Scores that are already on the scoreboard are never changed by starting a game.

Passphrase Forge

Each lodge forges a passphrase in its Warden Log and submits only its strength. The ranking shows on the scoreboard live. At the end, 1st gets +2 and 2nd gets +1.

Starting clears only the earlier Forge submissions, the same as Reset round on the Forge page.

Two-Key Rite

Each lodge types two different codes from its authenticator. Points are added to the scoreboard as lodges finish (1st +3, 2nd +2, everyone after +1).

Starting keeps the current scorecard. For a fresh race, use Reset scorecard on the Two-Key page first (it also removes this game's points from the scoreboard).

Other tools: Passphrase Forge page · Two-Key Rite page · Authenticator codes display
