The Silver Vault

Four locks. Four rune-words. You will need your eyes, your browser, and a little Roman history.

You found the door. Four locks guard the Silver Vault, and each rune-word is hidden somewhere a Warden has to look: behind a page, in a file for robots, in a fingerprint, and in plain sight. Open them all and the vault gives you a phrase to tell your counselor.

Stuck? Every lock has two hints. Use the small one first. There is no penalty for hints, but the best Wardens try first.
