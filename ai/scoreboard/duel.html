Prompt Duel

Press Start duel and squads send in their rewritten prompt and the output it produced (one entry per squad). When enough are in, press Reveal & open voting: the board shows the outputs as numbered cards with no squad names and Scouts vote from their Field Logs (never for their own squad, one vote each). Press Show results for the winner, then End duel to bring the scoreboard back. Award the style points on the scorekeeper page.
